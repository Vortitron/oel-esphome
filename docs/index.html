OEL for ESPHome

The animations from Pioneer's organic-EL car stereos, on an ESP32's display.

Pick a board and screen, choose clips from the bank below (hover one to play it), and copy the config. The clips are fetched from carozerra when ESPHome builds the firmware, so only the ones you pick go in. A sister project to After Dark for ESPHome.
